# CEREBRON Farm 19 — CYBER VIGIE Ω

Mission: unique defensive cybersecurity watch for CEREBRON. This farm fuses anti-hacker, anti-jailbreak/prompt-injection, model-security and CYBER-IQ measurement to avoid duplicate security agents.

## Position
F19 CYBER VIGIE -> GUARDIAN Ω -> CEREBRON validation -> AFAH

GUARDIAN remains superior. F19 cannot change hierarchy, permissions, safety policy, Guardian rules, or approve its own privilege elevation.

## Continuous watch domains
- repository/workflow/config integrity;
- IAM, tokens, secrets and least privilege;
- dependency/supply-chain risk;
- anomalous or unauthorized changes;
- prompt injection and jailbreak attempts;
- instruction/data boundary violations;
- model/tool takeover attempts;
- data poisoning and memory poisoning;
- suspicious cross-farm packets;
- exfiltration indicators;
- defensive incident containment and recovery.

## CYBER-IQ Ω
CYBER-IQ is a benchmark score, never a claim of intelligence.
Dimensions: DETECT, RESIST, CONTAIN, RECOVER, AUDIT.
Scores require reproducible defensive tests in authorized sandbox/fixtures.
No score may grant privileges.
